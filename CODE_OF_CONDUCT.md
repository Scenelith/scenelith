# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, spam, and publication of another person's credentials or private data are not accepted in Scenelith project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, users, or the project. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's security/contact channel rather than escalating them in public threads.
